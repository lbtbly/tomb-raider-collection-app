# Scout A: the Tomb Raider merchandise universe

Raw scout return, 2026-08-17. Synthesized into BRIEF.md under Research.

## 1. Categories, 1996 to 2026

- **Games**: main series across every platform re-release (TR1 1996 PS1/Saturn/PC
  through the remasters, Anniversary, Underworld, the 2013-2018 reboot trilogy,
  mobile spinoffs like Lara Croft GO, Guardian of Light, Temple of Osiris). Each
  carries regional box variants (PAL/NTSC/NTSC-J), Black Label vs Platinum and
  Greatest Hits reprints, Collector's/Limited/Survival Editions with steelbooks,
  art books, bundled figures.
- **Physical media extras**: press kits (Chronicles press kit with the Scarab of
  Darkness challenge disc), demo discs, magazine cover-mount CDs, preorder DLC
  cards.
- **Toys, figures, statues**: Playmates and ToyBiz 1990s Lara figures,
  Sideshow/Gaming Heads/PCS statues, Hasbro G.I. Joe x Lara Croft 30th-anniversary
  figure (2026), Tonner "Tomb Raider Legend" boxed doll, TUBBZ ducks, Funko Pop.
- **LEGO**: Ideas set concepts and fan proposals, never officially released as of
  2026. Worth a "never happened" state collectors still track.
- **Apparel**: Eidos/Square Enix Gear Store tees, Lucozade/Larazade promo shirts,
  convention staff shirts.
- **Comics**: Top Cow/Image (1999-2005), Dark Horse (2014-), IDW.
- **Novels**: Random House/Del Rey tie-in novels.
- **Films and home video**: 2001 and 2003 Paramount films, 2018 MGM/Warner reboot.
  VHS, DVD, Blu-ray steelbooks, prop replicas (pistols, the Writings of William
  Blake prop book, auctioned via Heritage), promotional lithographs.
- **Soundtracks and vinyl**: Nathan McCree 1996 score (first vinyl pressing 2025,
  Stone and Gold Marble variant), The Tomb Raider Suite 2xLP (2019), film
  soundtrack RSD 2xLP gold vinyl (2021), Shadow collector's double vinyl (2020),
  promo 12" samplers (2001).
- **Trading cards, board games, pinball**: licensed card sets, a Tomb Raider
  pinball machine.
- **Sponsorship tie-ins**: Lucozade/Larazade (1998, bottle-in-box promo pack),
  SEAT for the 2001 film, Land Rover Discovery TR-branded vehicle promotion,
  fast-food and cereal tie-ins in some territories.
- **Promotional, never sold**: E3 giveaways, cardboard standees, retailer preorder
  bonuses (GAME/GameStop exclusives), convention exclusives, press-only survival
  kits and badges (Shadow press kit), magazine subscription premiums (the French
  "Tomb Raider Official Fact Files" resin figurine partwork series).
- **Packaging variants**: Greatest Hits/Platinum reprints vs first-print Black
  Label, territory box art, censored vs uncensored covers in some markets.
- **Cosplay and prop tie-ins**: licensed replica prop weapons, licensed cosplay
  gear.

## 2. Size of the universe

No authoritative count exists. PriceCharting alone lists roughly 100 distinct
Tomb Raider game SKUs across platforms, regions, and editions. Adding statues,
figures, apparel, comics, novels, soundtracks, and promo items across 30 years
and multiple territories puts the realistic total at **1,500 to 4,000 distinct
catalogue entries** once regional and packaging variants split out. Comparable in
scale to a mid-size Discogs artist discography. No fan project has published a
complete count. That gap is the market.

## 4. Attributes collectors record and argue about

- **Region and release**: PAL vs NTSC vs NTSC-J, territory box art, language variants.
- **Print and label**: Black Label vs Platinum/Greatest Hits, first print vs reprint.
- **Edition name**: Standard / Collector's / Limited / Anniversary / Definitive /
  Survival, with exact bundled contents, since bundles varied by retailer and region.
- **Condition and completeness**: Sealed vs CIB (Complete In Box) vs loose, manual
  included, box wear, seal intact.
- **Grading**: WATA (now PSA Video Games) 10-point with separate box and seal
  grades (A++ to C for seal); VGA 100-point combined; CGC. Grading company,
  numeric grade, and certification serial are three distinct fields.
- **Provenance type**: retail (sold) vs promotional/press (never sold) vs
  convention exclusive vs preorder bonus. The sold-vs-given-away distinction is a
  first-class field, and it is exactly what the goal asks for.
- **Distributor and publisher era**: Eidos vs Square Enix vs Core Design vs
  Crystal Dynamics. Publisher rebrands feed authenticity questions.
- **Print run and numbering**: numbered editions (XXX/1000) on statues and vinyl.
- **Variant identifier**: SKU, catalogue number, barcode/UPC, matrix/runout for vinyl.
- **Year**: release year distinct from the copyright year printed on packaging.
  Collectors flag mismatches.

## 5. Pricing signal

- **PriceCharting**: structured per-platform game pricing (loose/CIB/new), daily,
  paid API.
- **eBay sold listings**: the primary real comp source, but as of 2025 the Finding
  API `findCompletedItems` is decommissioned. Current path is Browse API (active
  listings only, free) or Marketplace Insights API (sold data, 90-day window,
  requires Business-level approval). Plan for manual comp entry, not free sold data.
- **Discogs**: marketplace data for soundtrack vinyl and CDs, public API.
- **Heritage, Rock Island, Propstore, Hake's**: prop and high-end sales, searchable
  past-lot archives, no unified API. Citation quality, not bulk ingestion.
- **Catawiki**: low TR-specific volume, low priority.

## 6. Legal notes surfaced by this scout

- Square Enix Material Usage Policy permits non-commercial descriptive use of the
  Tomb Raider name, explicitly prohibits logo use and any implication of
  sponsorship. Text-only nominative references, no official logos or key art, and
  a visible takedown path.
  https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_US/documents/materialusagepolicy
- DSA: hosting user-generated content makes this a hosting service even at small
  scale. Notice-and-action mechanism required. Micro and small enterprises get
  lighter obligations, not full exemption. Scout D confirms specifics.
